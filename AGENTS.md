# Bicycle-Automotive Collisions

Vite + TanStack Start app. Domain: US bicycle-automotive collisions (`crashes` table / routes).

- Local DB: PGLite when `DATABASE_URL` unset
- Seed: `src/lib/crashes-seed.ts` (curated public US bicycle-automotive collisions; mirrored by `migrations/0004_real_crash_seed.sql` and `migrations/0005_lancaster_county_crash.sql`). Curated rows win when they overlap a bulk import.
- Bulk imports in `data/crash-imports/` (loaded once per database, not on every request):
  - **NHTSA FARS** pedalcyclist fatalities (`PER_TYP` 6 bicyclist and 7 other pedalcyclist, `INJ_SEV` 4). Latest complete annual files are 2022–2024 (2024 is the newest national CSV: 1,103 people). No names. `verified` is true. Refresh: `python3 scripts/fetch-fars-cyclists.py`. 2025 early estimates are not person-level and are not turned into rows.
  - **PennDOT** statewide bicycle crashes with a death or injury, 2022–2025, including Lancaster, York, Dauphin, Lebanon, Berks, and Chester. The public extract has year and month, not the calendar day, and no names. `verified` is true. Refresh: `python3 scripts/fetch-penndot-bike.py`.
  - **News queue**: Google News RSS (GDELT DOC when it is not rate-limiting) for cyclist killed/injured headlines. Only an explicit place and outcome are stored. `verified` is false and `review_status` is `pending`, so desk totals stay on the official and curated rows. Refresh: `node scripts/fetch-news-crashes.mjs`. Promote with `node scripts/promote-news-crash.mjs <id>`.
  - Load into Postgres: `node scripts/load-crash-imports.mjs`. PGLite loads the same files at startup. `npm run import:fars`, `import:penndot`, `import:news`, and `import:load` are the shortcuts.
- Theme: blue-dominant tokens in `src/styles.css`
- Traffic: `/traffic` uses FHWA HPMS AADT + posted speed (`geo.dot.gov`) outside the Lancaster region. That region is Lancaster, York, Dauphin, Lebanon, Berks, and Chester counties (`src/lib/penndot-region.mjs`), using PennDOT RMS volumes, posted speed, and shoulder width, plus the local-road inventory (`scripts/fetch-penndot-lancaster.py`, bundled in `public/traffic-corridors.json`). Cecil and Harford, Maryland, are adjacent and not included. Clicking a segment opens name, vehicles per day, posted speed, and shoulder width when published. Region colors: PennDOT ACCESS_CTRL 1 (Limited access) and FHWA interstate/freeway classes are red; volume over 5,000 vehicles per day is red and beats the wide-shoulder cap; a shoulder wider than 4 ft with a posted speed under 45 mph stays amber on other roads; speed over 40 with volume over 2,000 and under 5,000 and a shoulder under 4 ft is amber; red is otherwise both volume and speed in the upper 20%; amber if shoulder is under 2 ft, speed is over 40 mph, or volume is over 1,000; volume under 400 is green only when amber and red do not apply.
- Bike lanes: OpenStreetMap dedicated cycleways in `public/bike-lanes.json`, drawn on every map (teal, on by default)
